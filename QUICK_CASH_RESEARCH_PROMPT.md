# Quick Cash & Gig Research Protocol

**OBJECTIVE:** Find and compile a list of **5-10 LEGITIMATE** quick-cash, gig, or micro-task opportunities.
**CRITICAL:** Focus on platforms and tasks that pay consistently and have a low barrier to entry. Verified payouts are a must.

## 1. Search Strategy
Use Google, Reddit (r/beermoney, r/workonline), and specialized gig economy forums.
*   **Keywords:** "Instant pay surveys", "Microtasks", "User testing", "Data entry gigs", "Sign up bonus", "Mystery shopping", "Get paid to...", "Side hustle app".
*   **Filtering:** Avoid scams (check Trustpilot/Reddit reviews). Look for "Low minimum withdrawal" or "Instant checkout".

## 2. Requirement: Structured List
Return the results as a **Structured Markdown List**. For EACH opportunity, provide:

1.  **Title:** (e.g., "User Testing for Websites" or "Paid Survey on Consumer Habits")
2.  **Platform/Provider:** (Name of the app or website)
3.  **Category:** (MANDATORY: Pick ONE from this specific list: SURVEY, USER_TESTING, DATA_LABELING, MYSTERY_SHOPPING, GIG, SIDE_HUSTLE, AFFILIATE, GAME_TESTING, TRANSCRIPTION, WATCH_TO_EARN, CASHBACK, DIGITAL_TASK, BUG_BOUNTY, FOCUS_GROUP)
4.  **Sign-up/Task URL:** (DIRECT link to the specific task or sign-up page. MANDATORY.)
5.  **Time Commitment:** (e.g., "15 mins", "1 hour", "Ongoing")
6.  **Payout Speed & Method:** (e.g., "Instant via PayPal", "Weekly Direct Deposit", "Gift Cards within 24h")
7.  **Estimated Earnings:** (e.g., "$10 per test", "$0.50 - $2.00 per survey", "$15/hr")
8.  **Requirements:** (e.g., "Must be 18+", "Requires Microphone", "Android Phone needed", "Tiktok Account")
9.  **Detailed Description:** (Explain exactly WHAT the user has to do. Is it recording their screen? Answering questions? Taking photos of store shelves? Be specific about the "how-to" part.) - minimum 150 words.
10. **Image URL:** (Logo of the platform or app icon. Ensure it's a direct image link.)
